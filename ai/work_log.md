# Work log

Reverse-chronological record of **completed work**. Not a chat transcript — record intent, actions, and outcomes.

## How to append

Copy the template; fill every field you know. Keep entries short enough to scan in under a minute.

```
### YYYY-MM-DD — <short title>
- **Status:** done | partial | reverted
- **Goal:** why
- **Done:** what changed (paths)
- **Result:** what is now true / how verified
- **Follow-ups:** optional
```
